Zero-Trust Patient Security Standard

Privacy, Cryptography & Compliance

Healthcare information is among the most sensitive data in existence. AI Smart Healthcare is engineered with zero-compromise encryption, role isolation, and patient autonomy at its very foundation.

AES-256 Symmetric Encryption

All patient symptom logs, medical history, diagnoses, diet, and lifestyle prescriptions are encrypted at rest using Fernet (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256 authenticated encryption).

PBKDF2 Password Salting

Credentials are never stored in plain text. Passwords undergo 100,000 iterations of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with cryptographically random 16-byte unique salts.

Right to be Forgotten

In compliance with GDPR Article 17 and HIPAA data sovereignty, you can permanently erase every trace of your consultation logs and profile with a single click.
